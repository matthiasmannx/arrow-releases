# Privacy – Arrow (iPhone and Mac)

Arrow is a web browser made by Matthias Mann. This page explains what happens with your data.

## What Arrow collects
Nothing. Arrow has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. The developer receives no data about you or about the sites you visit.

## What stays on your device
- Browsing data (cookies, site storage, open tabs) is stored only on your device, separated per space.
- Site icons are downloaded once from the sites themselves and cached on your device.
- The key to your password vault is kept in the iOS Keychain and filling in a password asks for Face ID.

## What syncs through your own iCloud Drive
If you choose the folder "Arrow" in iCloud Drive, Arrow reads and writes these files there, in your own iCloud account:
- spaces, favorites, pinned tabs and settings (`arrow-sync.json`)
- the list of tabs open on each device (`arrow-tabs-….json`)
- optionally your encrypted password vault (`wachtwoorden.kluis`, AES-256, protected by a sync password only you know)

These files are never sent to the developer.

## Connections Arrow makes itself
- The sites you open.
- Search suggestions while you type in the command bar, requested from your chosen search engine (Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo; other engines: none). You can turn this off in Settings on the iPhone and on the Mac.
- Files you choose to download, and a site's own icon (once per site).
- A public list of advertising and tracking domains (pgl.yoyo.org), refreshed about once a week, used to block ads on your device.

## Permissions
Camera, microphone and location are only used when a website asks for them and you allow it.
